# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in Gear S3 Explorer welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private contact channel.
